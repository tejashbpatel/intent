My reminder did not arrive

Check these in order:

1. On your iPhone, open Settings, then Notifications, then Intent, and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

2. Check that a Focus mode (like Do Not Disturb or Sleep) is not silencing it.

3. Open the task in Intent and make sure it still has a reminder time in the future.

How do I use Siri with Intent?

Say "Hey Siri, capture in Intent" and speak your task, for example "go to dance practice today at 7:30 PM". You can also find the action in the Shortcuts app and assign it to the Action Button.

If Siri does not seem to know about Intent, open Intent once, wait a minute, and try again.

How do I use "Got a free moment?"

When you add a task, choose roughly how long it takes. Then tap one of the time tiles on the Today screen to see what fits. Tap the play button next to a task to start a countdown.

What does "Let it go" do?

Tasks that have been waiting two weeks or more show a Let it go button. It moves the task to the Let-go shelf, which you can open from the bottom of the Today screen. You can bring any task back from there.

A festival date looks wrong

Festival dates follow the lunar calendar and can differ by a day between regions. If a date does not match what your family follows, email us and we will check it. You can also turn festival notifications off in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

Your tasks stay on your phone. Intent has no account and no sign-up. Deleting the app deletes your tasks, so the data is not recoverable afterwards. See our Privacy Policy for details.
