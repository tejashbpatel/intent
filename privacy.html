INTENT Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

INTENT ("the app") helps you remember what you meant to do. Your privacy matters, and the app is designed to keep your information on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. INTENT has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

The intentions you create (titles, notes, categories, emojis, dates and reminders) are stored only on your device. A small summary of your upcoming intentions is shared between the app and its Home Screen widget on the same device, using an App Group. It never leaves your device.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to capture an intention by voice, INTENT asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your speech is converted to text using Apple's Speech framework, which may send audio to Apple to process it, under Apple's privacy policy. INTENT does not record or keep your audio. Only the text you confirm is saved on your device. You can turn these permissions off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

“Voice capture uses Apple’s built-in speech recognition. On supported devices this happens on your phone; Intent does not collect or store your audio.”

Notifications

INTENT uses local notifications to remind you at the times you choose. These are scheduled on your device and do not involve any server.

Children

INTENT does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
